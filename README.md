A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Tuesday
24 November 2026 — 13 stories from 8 regions, rendered programmatically
with Python and PIL.

## The day's stories

Lead: a Russian strike kills 2 and wounds 22 in Zaporizhzhia, days after
Ukraine said it sank the Russian warship Merkury. Also: the US blacklists
an Alibaba AI unit as Jensen Huang returns to Nvidia; an explosion kills
6 in Hanoi; Seoul summons Russia's defence attaché; Iraq takes over
Kurdistan's border crossings; Japan pays its ICC bill early; and the
ousted Sheikh Hasina plans to return to Bangladesh.

## How it works

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` (1024×1024, rendered with Python/PIL) to the
grader's output directory. The image is deterministic: rendering it twice
produces byte-identical output.

`entry.png`: 129,884 bytes,
sha256 `8425b13dc86e002cd82d51f0e01ff69455a926409d15eea4490d920778241e04`.

This is v2, built for the round-2 blind cutoff. It replaces v1 with
Tuesday's newer developments: the overnight Zaporizhzhia strike leads,
the South Korea and Venezuela stories carry their Tuesday follow-ups,
and the Hanoi blast, Iraq border takeover, Japan's ICC payment, Hasina's
planned return and the record quasar are new.
